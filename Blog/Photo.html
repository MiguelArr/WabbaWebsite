Camera gear details

I use a Lumix LX10, I got it because it seemed to be one of the best compact cameras and so far it has been super easy to use. It's small and pocketable and gives you all the control you need, you can't change its lens and I'm interested in really zoomed out and zoomed in photography so who knows if I'll delve into that at some point. So far I'm not really thinking about it and just enjoying the process.

Some pictures were also taken with my phone lol.
